## Findings, severity-ordered

- **P0:** none.
- **P1:** none.
- **P2-1 — the headline claim is overstated; the disposition it supports is
  nevertheless correct.** The worker reports that the designated highest-risk
  question "resolves affirmatively". What is actually proved is narrower.
  `arithmeticLoweringAvailable` (`BoundedArithmetic.lean:397`) concludes only
  `mulFuel w 0 a b = a * b ∧ (divmodFuel w a b).1 = a / b` — facts about two
  Lean-level `Nat` recursions. The instruction blocks are proved separately to
  have admissible immediates (`[2,2,2,2]` and `[2,2,2,0,2,2]`, by `rfl`) and
  tags within the frozen twelve. **No theorem connects execution of
  `mulByRegisterBlock` / `divByRegisterBlock` to those recursions.** That is
  the shape of the R2 "disconnected opcode lists" failure mode named in the
  dossier's table: tags plus lengths are not an executable program.
  Mitigating, and the reason this is P2 rather than P1: the worker's own
  docstring states "the residual work is the operational lowering proof, which
  is open, not impossible", every affected matrix row is marked `PENDING`, and
  no `consume*` surface claims the connection. So the candidate does not assert
  the gap away.
  **I agree with the disposition of not reporting `OBSTRUCTED`.** Declining to
  declare an obstruction requires only that the lowering not be provably
  impossible, and a syntactically admissible candidate whose intended
  arithmetic is proved correct clears that bar. But the correct status of the
  question is **UNRESOLVED-BUT-PROMISING**, not resolved, and the successor
  contract must not inherit "resolved" as a premise.
- **P2-2 — the R2 matrix has no freeze-to-final preservation mechanism for its
  own locally frozen rows.** The R1 matrix delimited editable regions with
  `<!-- EVIDENCE-EDITABLE-BEGIN/END -->` and `<!-- STATUS-EDITABLE-BEGIN/END -->`
  and normalised them before comparing freeze to HEAD. The R2 matrix contains
  **zero HTML comment markers**, and its replay's `Test-FrozenRows` instead
  compares candidate rows byte-for-byte against the *accepted source blobs*
  (`9eb1625a…` for four inherited architecture rows, `be80468e…` for the 42
  registry rows). That substitution is **stronger** where it applies — it
  anchors on immutable accepted inputs rather than on the worker's own freeze —
  but it leaves the ~43 R2-local rows (`B3-HIST-*`, `INV-*`, `REPLAY-*`) with
  no preservation check of any kind, because no accepted blob contains them.
  A local frozen requirement could therefore be softened during implementation
  and nothing in the candidate would detect it.
  I reconstructed the missing check independently. Of 43 local rows present at
  freeze, **38 requirement cells are byte-identical** at the candidate. The
  five that differ are all `REPLAY-*` rows, and in every case the frozen
  requirement text is preserved **verbatim as a suffix**, prefixed with a
  status marker (`**IMPLEMENTED (PASS).** `, and for `REPLAY-EXACT-REGISTRY`
  the honest `**IDENTITY CHECKS IMPLEMENTED; SEMANTIC CAMPAIGN NOT
  IMPLEMENTED.** `). The cause is structural, not evasive: that two-column
  table has no evidence or status column, so the only place to record evidence
  was inside the frozen cell. **No requirement was weakened.** The defect is
  the absent mechanism, not a misuse of it.
- **P3-1 — replay `REGISTRY-VERDICT-COUNTS` cannot pass as written**

- **P3-2 — the semantic mutation campaign was not executed and

## Commendations

Three things the worker did that the contract wanted and that are easy to skip:

1. **It disclosed a witness that does not discriminate.** `pinnedTargetSubPhase`
   was frozen at PC 32 as directed, but the worker reported that the operands
   there are `(1,1)` — the degenerate `left = right` case where truncated and
   ordered semantics coincide — so it does not test the repair, and supplied a
   strictly ordered alternative (tick 54, PC 55, operands `(2,1)`). The
   contract asked it to determine this by execution and record it either way;
   it did, and chose the reading that weakens its own evidence.
2. **It quantified `B3-HIST-16` non-vacuity** rather than asserting it: 64
   underflows out of 727 reached subtractions. That converts "underflow is
   reachable" into "an ordered-subtraction premise is false on the accepted
   execution", which is the difference between a repair and a weakening.
3. **It fixed the named R1 regression and verified it in the failing
   direction**, retaining a bare-`lean` negative control, and it surfaced three
   further harness defects — including a false clean-tree `PASS` caused by
   empty PowerShell event-based capture, which is precisely the class of defect
   that makes a harness unsound rather than merely broken.
